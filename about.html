About Tellyro.

Tellyro is a streaming subscription service with real people behind the support chat. This page explains what we offer, how it works and how to reach us.

What Tellyro is

Tellyro is the name used for our streaming service: live TV channels, movies and series for viewers in Europe and around the world, on the devices you already own. You choose a plan for 1, 2 or 3 devices at the same time, and our team helps you get set up.

Channel and library numbers come from our service providers, are a guide, and can change over time.

How it works

  1. Choose a plan, or request a free trial and tell us a little about your setup.
  2. Our team sends your login details on WhatsApp or Telegram when your trial or subscription is activated. Keep them private.
  3. Follow the installation guide for your device. If you get stuck, message us and we will walk you through it.

Payments and refunds

Payments are arranged only through our WhatsApp or Telegram support chat. There is no online checkout on this website. Refund requests can be made within the first 3 days, and your statutory consumer rights are not affected. The details are in our Terms of Service.

Who runs Tellyro

Tellyro is the name used for this service. If you need the provider’s registered legal details, ask our team before you buy. How we handle your personal information is explained in our Privacy Policy.

Apps and guides

Tellyro doesn’t make the player apps in our installation guides. We recommend free apps from their developers and link to the official store pages, so you can check who published each app before you install it. Our Troubleshooting Blog has step-by-step guides for common TV and app problems.
