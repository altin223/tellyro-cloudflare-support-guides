    Common installation questions

    Where do I get my login details?

    Your server URL, username and password or M3U link are supplied by Tellyro support after activation or a trial. This website never asks you to type your credentials into its help widget.

    Why doesn't my app show channels?

    Check that the account is active, restart the player and refresh the playlist. Confirm the login format and spelling. If channels are still missing, contact support.

    Do I pay for the player too?

    Some third-party player apps are free with optional upgrades; others may require separate app activation. Player fees are not included unless support explicitly confirms otherwise.
