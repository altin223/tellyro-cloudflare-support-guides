01Who we are & how to contact us

This policy explains how the Tellyro website and support team handle personal information. Tellyro is the name used for this service. For privacy questions, access requests or complaints, contact our team using the official WhatsApp or Telegram links on this page. If you need the provider’s registered legal details, request them before purchasing.

02Information we collect

When you request a trial or contact support, you may provide your name, email address, country, device type, viewing preferences, message and other details necessary to answer your request. If you subscribe, support may also handle the relevant account, subscription, activation and renewal information.

When you visit the website, hosting and security providers may process technical data such as your IP address, browser, approximate location derived from IP, security events and page requests. The website does not ask you to enter card information into its trial form.

03Why we use this information

We use contact and account details to respond to enquiries, arrange requested trials or subscriptions, deliver support and manage activations or renewals. Where applicable, these activities are necessary to take steps at your request before a contract or to perform a contract. We also use limited technical information to secure and operate the website based on legitimate interests, and retain records when a legal obligation requires it.

We do not send optional marketing messages without a lawful basis and any consent required by local law.

04Payments and third-party communications

Payments are arranged through our support channels, not through an on-site checkout. Payment services and messaging platforms you choose may collect information according to their own policies. Please do not send full card numbers, passwords or other sensitive credentials through the trial form or support chat.

05Service providers and sharing

We may use infrastructure and security providers, including Cloudflare, to deliver and protect the website. The site uses Google Fonts, which may involve requests to Google, and may use form-processing or verification services if configured. If you choose WhatsApp or Telegram, your communication is also handled by that platform. Access to information should be limited to providers and team members who need it for these purposes.

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers.

06Cookies and local storage

This website uses your browser’s local storage to remember your language and session storage for certain on-site notices or offers. Cloudflare or another security provider may set cookies needed for security checks. If we later enable optional analytics or advertising technologies, we will update this notice and request consent where required. You can clear local storage and cookies in your browser settings.

07How long data is kept

We keep requests and support records only as long as reasonably necessary to answer you, administer subscriptions, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal obligations. Records required for financial or regulatory reasons may be retained longer. The precise period depends on the record and the applicable legal requirements; you can ask support about your specific information.

08International processing and security

Tellyro serves people in several countries, and support tools and service providers may process information outside your home country. Where required, appropriate safeguards for international transfers should be in place. We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

09Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or receive a copy of your personal data, object to certain processing and withdraw consent where we rely on it. To exercise a right, contact support and describe your request. We may need to verify your identity before responding. You may also complain to the appropriate data protection regulator in your country.

10Children and changes to this policy

Our subscriptions are intended for adults who can enter into a contract. We do not knowingly solicit trial requests from children. We may update this policy to reflect changes in the website, its providers or applicable law. The effective date shown above will be changed when a material update is published.

Our support team can help you understand these details.
